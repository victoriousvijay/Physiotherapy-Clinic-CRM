Neurological Physiotherapy

Targeted movement support, balance re-education, and functional independence for neurological conditions.

Harnessing Neuroplasticity for Movement

Neurological conditions such as stroke, Parkinson's disease, peripheral neuropathy, or nerve compression require specialized physical therapy techniques aimed at retraining brain-to-muscle nerve pathways through neuroplasticity.

  • Gait, posture, and balance re-education
  • Spasticity management & joint tightness prevention
  • Functional motor task retraining for daily independence
  • Caregiver orientation & safe transfer techniques
Responsible Care Note: Neurological physiotherapy provides functional physical rehabilitation and movement support. It is conducted alongside your consulting neurologist's medical treatment.
