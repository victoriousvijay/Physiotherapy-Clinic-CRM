Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Your privacy and medical confidentiality are paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines how Dr. Arti Tanwar's physiotherapy practice collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when requesting a consultation, including your full name, phone number, email address, locality, preferred appointment dates, and brief descriptions of your mobility concerns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to respond to your booking requests, confirm session schedules via Phone/WhatsApp, organize home visits, and maintain internal patient communication. We do NOT sell, lease, or share your data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security

All lead data submitted through our website forms is encrypted in transit and securely stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore with restricted role-based admin controls.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your contact records at any time by emailing care@pulsemotionphysio.com.
