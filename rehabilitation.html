Post-Fracture Rehabilitation

Progressive joint mobilization, stiffness reduction, and strength rebuilding after bone healing and cast removal.

Rebuilding Flexibility & Muscle Strength

After weeks of immobilization in a cast or splint, joints become stiff and surrounding muscles undergo disuse atrophy. Structured post-fracture rehabilitation is vital to safely restore full range of motion, break down scar tissue, and retrain weight-bearing confidence.

  • Gentle manual joint mobilization & soft-tissue release
  • Progressive weight-bearing & gait re-education
  • Targeted resistance exercises for weak muscle groups
  • Swelling management & tissue circulation techniques
