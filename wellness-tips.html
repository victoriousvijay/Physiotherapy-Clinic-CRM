Wellness, Lifestyle & Hydration Guidance

Supporting your physical rehabilitation with optimal hydration habits, sleep ergonomics, and tissue wellness.

Hydration & Cartilage Elasticity

Intervertebral discs in the spine and joint cartilage are composed predominantly of water. Adequate fluid intake throughout the day maintains disc height, cushions spinal loading, and optimizes tissue healing speed.

Key Lifestyle Pillars for Joint Health:

  • Daily hydration (2.5 - 3 liters) to keep spinal discs supple
  • Sleep position support: pillow between knees for side sleepers
  • Nutrient support: calcium, vitamin D3, and protein for tissue repair
  • Anti-inflammatory food habits (turmeric, omega-3, leafy greens)
